Studio Ethos

Architecture meets interior sensibility.

"The void is not empty space — it is the volume where life, light, and material converge."

Founded in Islamabad, The Fifth Wall was built on a fundamental belief: architecture and interior design must never be split into isolated steps. When one creative mind conceives both the outer envelope and the inner atmosphere, every window frame frames a deliberate sightline, every wall welcomes natural daylight, and every material feels organic to the structure.

From signature private residences and luxury villas in Islamabad and Rawalpindi to bespoke architectural and commercial commissions nationwide across Pakistan, we guide clients from initial zoning concepts to the final coat of finish.

Unified Rigour
Structure, light, and joinery resolved under one single architectural oversight.

Methodology

How Every Project Unfolds.

A transparent four-phase process designed to provide clarity, budget security, and architectural excellence at every turn.

01

Discovery

Phase 1 · 1–2 Weeks

Initial site meeting, lifestyle or brand brief analysis, zoning constraints review, and preliminary budget framework.

02

Concept & 3D

Phase 2 · 2–3 Weeks

Architectural massing, spatial layouts, material samples, and photorealistic 3D renders confirming the visual direction.

03

Documentation

Phase 3 · 3–4 Weeks

Detailed construction drawings, CDA/RDA permits, structural, plumbing, and electrical coordination, and finish schedules.

04

Execution

Phase 4 · On-Site

Rigorous on-site quality inspections, contractor management, bespoke joinery installation, snag clearing, and handover.

Yes. All our architectural design packages include full compliance with regulatory authority bylaws, structural vetting documentation, and submission drawing sets prepared specifically for CDA, RDA, DHA, or housing society approvals.
When architecture and interior design are separated, costly clashes inevitably arise — electrical points conflict with custom wardrobes, windows cut into ceiling designs, or HVAC ducts destroy room proportions. The Fifth Wall resolves architecture and interiors concurrently, saving substantial time and avoiding costly construction modifications.
The design and approval phase typically spans 4 to 8 weeks depending on the project's scale and complexity. For full turnkey construction and luxury fit-out, execution timelines generally range between 6 to 14 months with fixed milestone schedules.
