01 / ARCHITECTURE

Architectural Planning & Permits

From site zoning evaluations and orientation analysis to full construction working sets. We prepare all structural calculations, MEP coordination drawings, and municipal submission sets to obtain formal approvals without regulatory delays.

Deliverables & Documentation:

CDA & RDA Permit Drawings DHA & Bahria Submission Sets Structural Engineering Plans Plumbing & Electrical Layouts Soil Testing & Foundation Specs Full Construction Working Sets

02 / INTERIORS

Turnkey Interior Architecture

We design interior volumes that breathe. Ceiling heights, custom pocket doors, architectural recesses, imported travertine and Italian marble dry-lays, layered ambient illumination, and bespoke master suites curated for comfort and sophistication.

Deliverables & Documentation:

Reflected Ceiling Plans (RCP) Marble Dry-Lay & Tile Layouts Architectural Lighting Schedules Luxury Kitchen & Bath Detailing HVAC & Concealed Duct Planning Turnkey Material Specifications

03 / VISUALIZATION

Photorealistic 3D CGI & Renders

Eliminate guesswork before breaking ground. We generate high-definition photorealistic 3D renders that simulate actual solar angles in Islamabad, exact facade marble veining, nighttime illumination schemes, and internal sightlines.

Deliverables & Documentation:

4K Facade Visualizations Day & Twilight Atmospheric Studies Photorealistic Interior CGI Material & Texture Palettes 3D Virtual Walkthroughs

04 / LANDSCAPE

Landscape & Terrace Architecture

Treating the exterior envelope with the same architectural discipline as living rooms. We design courtyard reflections, infinity pools, integrated planters, sunken conversation pits, and rooftop pergolas optimized for Islamabad's seasons.

Deliverables & Documentation:

Hardscaping & Stone Paving Courtyard & Terrace Garden Plans Swimming Pool & Water Features Pergolas & Shading Canopies Exterior Architectural Lighting

05 / BESPOKE JOINERY

Bespoke Millwork & FF&E

Off-the-shelf retail furniture rarely fits tailored architectural volumes. We design custom built-in wardrobes, monolithic credenzas, concealed television walls, and custom dining sets fabricated from solid oak, ash, and imported brass fittings.

Deliverables & Documentation:

Custom Joinery Shop Drawings Wardrobe & Vanity Specifications Solid Wood & Metal Detailing Curated Luxury Furniture Sourcing Hardware & Fixture Schedules

06 / SITE OVERSIGHT

Turnkey Construction Supervision

Designs remain drawings unless executed with uncompromising field oversight. We conduct periodic site inspections, verify rebar sizing before concrete pours, audit contractor BOQs against actual work, and resolve technical snags on site.

Deliverables & Documentation:

Weekly On-Site Inspections Structural Pour Certifications BOQ Bill Auditing & Cost Control Snag Lists & Quality Rectifications Turnkey Handover Verification
