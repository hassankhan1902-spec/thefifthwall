Financial Framework

What Drives Your Turnkey Cost per sqft?

We believe in complete transparency. Residential investment across Pakistan is driven by four primary architectural tiers. Here is how your capital is deployed across the build cycle:

01 / Foundation & Structure

Engineering & Grey Works

Earthquake-resistant structural concrete, certified Grade-60 steel, and water-tight subterranean damp-proofing.

  • Mughal / Amreli Grade-60 Deformed Steel
  • OPC Grade Cement & Screened Sand
  • Engineered Raft Foundation & Retaining Walls
  • Termite Prevention & Bitumen Tanking
02 / Building Systems

MEP & Climate Control

Long-term durability depends on concealed conduits, high-pressure piping, and efficient HVAC distribution.

  • Class-A PPRC & UPVC Plumbing Lines
  • Pakistan Cables Multi-Strand Electrical
  • Concealed VRF HVAC Piping & Ducts
  • Solar-Ready Inverter Conduits & DBs
03 / Envelope & Glazing

Thermal Facades & Glass

Energy efficiency and acoustic insulation against Margalla weather through double-glazed floor-to-ceiling glass.

  • Thermal-Break Aluminum Framing
  • Double-Glazed Low-E Argon-Filled Glass
  • Fair-Face Architectural Concrete Plasters
  • Natural Stone / Travertine Rainscreens
04 / Interior Craftsmanship

Bespoke Millwork & Stone

Hand-finished walnut, ash veneer joinery, imported Italian marble floorings, and soft ambient architectural illumination.

  • Imported Statuario / Botticino / Travertine
  • Solid Wood Core Doors & Blum Hardware
  • Custom Fitted Wardrobes & Island Kitchens
  • Grohe / Kohler Concealed Sanitary Fixtures

Our online estimator is calibrated against live contractor tenders, material procurement logs, and market rates across major urban centers in Pakistan (Islamabad, Rawalpindi, Lahore, and premier societies). It provides an accurate ±8% benchmark for turnkey delivery. Following our initial site and design discovery meeting, we deliver a line-item Bill of Quantities (BOQ) with fixed prices.

Grey Structure (~48% of budget) covers excavation, foundation, concrete framing (Grade 60 rebar & OPC cement), brick masonry, plastering, subterranean waterproofing, and concealed plumbing/electrical piping.

Turnkey Finishes (~52% of budget) encompasses the luxury architectural experience: imported marble/porcelain tiling, bespoke solid oak cabinetry, thermal-break double glazing, designer sanitary fixtures, architectural lighting, and bespoke interior millwork.
