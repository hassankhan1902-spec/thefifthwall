When embarking on an architectural residence in Pakistan, prospective commissioners face a crucial strategic choice: award the construction contract as a Turnkey execution (Architecture + Grey + Finishes managed under one roof) or hire separate sub-contractors on an item-rate grey structure arrangement.

## The Illusion of Cheap Labor Rates

Many homeowners are initially seduced by contractors quoting low square-foot rates for grey structure (e.g. ₨ 2,800 to ₨ 3,200 per sqft). However, these item-rate quotes almost always exclude scaffolding, water-proofing additives, electrical conduit quality guarantees, and steel rebar wastage margins.

> "When design and construction are severed, contractors blame the architect for complex drawings, and the architect blames the contractor for sloppy tolerances. Turnkey delivery unifies accountability."

## The Turnkey Advantage: Guaranteed Price & Specification

In a comprehensive turnkey contract with The Fifth Wall Studio, our engineering team manages the complete Bills of Quantities (BOQ) with milestone-based payment schedules tied to independent structural inspections:

* **Mughal / Amreli Grade 60 Steel:** Zero compromise on structural rebar yield strength.
* **Fauji / Bestway OPC Cement:** Batch-tested cylinder crushing tests for roof slabs and foundations.
* **Shadow-Gap Joinery & Level-5 Skim Coating:** Executed by specialized studio master craftsmen rather than daily casual labor.
