Islamabad is situated in proximity to major fault zones, placing the twin cities within Seismic Zone 2B and Zone 3 under the Building Code of Pakistan. This geographical reality makes structural engineering an artistic partner to architectural expression.

## Designing the Floating Cantilever

Clients often admire our floating concrete stairs and 10-foot second-storey bedroom projections. Achieving these daring structural gestures without awkward ground columns requires post-tensioned beam design, high-strength concrete mixes (3,000 to 4,000 PSI), and continuous rebar anchorage into central shear cores.

Every Fifth Wall design undergoes rigorous finite element analysis (ETABS) modeling to verify wind load resistance, soil bearing capacity, and seismic ductility before a single shovel enters the earth.
