For decades, residential luxury in Pakistan was synonymous with high-gloss porcelain tiles, heavy gold moldings, and faux ceilings. The contemporary architectural renaissance in Islamabad embraces the quiet power of organic, tactile materiality.

## The Beauty of Indigenous Stone

Pakistan possesses some of the world's most remarkable natural stone quarries. Honed Ziarat White from Balochistan, Taunsa limestone, and Khuzdar onyx possess organic veining that rivals Italian Carrara when cut and book-matched with architectural precision.

> "When you run your hand across a honed travertine vanity wall or open a floor-to-ceiling walnut pocket door, you immediately feel the difference between authentic craftsmanship and superficial veneers."

## Acoustic Warmth with Custom Millwork

Polished marble floors reflect sound harshness. To create serene, museum-quality acoustic warmth, our interior studio balances stone floors with slatted acoustic walnut paneling, fluted timber dividers, and textured mineral limewash plaster.
