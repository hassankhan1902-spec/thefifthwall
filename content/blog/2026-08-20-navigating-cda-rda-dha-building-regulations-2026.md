Securing municipal approval from the Capital Development Authority (CDA), Rawalpindi Development Authority (RDA), or Defense Housing Authority (DHA) is a critical milestone before any excavation begins. Minor deviations in setback lines or covered area calculations can halt construction for months.

## Critical Byelaw Thresholds:

* **Maximum Height Limits:** DHA standard residential villas allow 30′ to 35′ total structure height from road crest, whereas CDA sectors (F-6, F-7, F-8, E-7) permit specific mumty and parapet variations based on sector zoning.
* **Basement Footprint:** Basements must strictly respect boundary setbacks; building beyond the sanctioned plinth line risks heavy non-compoundable demolition notices.
* **Solar & Rainwater Harvesting:** 2026 regulations mandate rooftop rainwater percolation pits and dedicated solar conduit pathways for residential plots over 10 Marla.
